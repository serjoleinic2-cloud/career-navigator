TITLE: WORLD MAP ORIENTATION FIX (BOTTOM → TOP)

PRIORITY: CRITICAL

GOAL:
The player must feel like they are climbing upward through the world.
Current implementation scrolls and progresses from TOP to BOTTOM, which creates the wrong psychological effect.

REQUIREMENTS

1. Reverse world orientation.

Player starts at the BOTTOM of the world.

All progression moves UPWARD.

Lowest node = first mission.

Highest node = final destination.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Camera position.

On entering WorldRenderer:

- Camera must focus on the player's current node.
- Player should appear in the lower third of the screen.
- Show only a small part of the upcoming path.
- Do NOT reveal the whole map.

--------------------------------------------------

3. Path direction.

Every connection must visually lead upward.

Allowed:
↖ ↑ ↗

Not allowed:
↓
↙
↘

--------------------------------------------------

4. World scrolling.

Scrolling toward higher progress moves upward.

The world must never feel like descending.

--------------------------------------------------

5. Unlock animation.

After completing a mission:

- Unlock next node.
- Camera smoothly moves upward.
- Previous node remains below.
- New node appears above.

Animation:
Duration: 700–900 ms
Ease: easeOutCubic

--------------------------------------------------

6. Node positioning.

All chapter nodes must have increasing Y coordinates.

Example:

START
↓

Chapter 1

↓

Chapter 2

↓

Interview

↓

Offer

Internally Y values may increase or decrease depending on coordinate system, but VISUALLY the player always climbs upward.

--------------------------------------------------

7. Verify all calculations.

Check:

- node layout
- camera offset
- scroll direction
- focus logic
- active node positioning

Remove any inversion that causes downward progression.

--------------------------------------------------

EXPECTED RESULT

Player starts at the bottom.

Career progression always feels like climbing.

Camera follows upward movement.

No screen should visually imply descending.
