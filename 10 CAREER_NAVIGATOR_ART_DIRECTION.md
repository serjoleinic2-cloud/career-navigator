# CAREER NAVIGATOR
# ART DIRECTION BIBLE

Version 1.0

---

# Philosophy

Career Navigator is NOT a business application.

Career Navigator is NOT a game.

Career Navigator is a journey.

The player must feel that every completed task changes the world around him.

The world is a visual metaphor of professional growth.

The interface must never remind the player of spreadsheets, HR software or learning platforms.

The player should feel that he is travelling.

---

# Core Feeling

The application should create five emotions.

Calm

Hope

Progress

Curiosity

Achievement

Never:

Stress

Noise

Information overload

Corporate feeling

---

---

# World Structure

The world is always vertical.

Bottom

Beginning.

Middle

Learning.

Top

Mastery.

Player always moves upward.

Never downward.

---

# World Composition

The world is NOT a single illustration.

It consists of independent layers.

Sky

Mountains

Clouds

Atmosphere

Background Islands

Foreground Islands

Bridges

Particles

Light

HUD

The artwork must never contain HUD.

HUD is always rendered by the application.

---

# Camera

The camera never jumps.

Only:

slow pan

slow zoom

slow rise

The player should feel carried by the world.

---

Each profession contains:

---

# Non-negotiable Rules

The world always grows upward.

The player always moves forward.

The world reacts to success.

The HUD never becomes the world.

The artwork never contains gameplay.

Every profession has its own visual identity.

Every screen must feel like the same universe.

The user should recognize Career Navigator from a single screenshot.

Golden Rule №1

The World always owns the screen.

Никогда не должно существовать большого контейнера, который визуально отделяет интерфейс от мира.

Golden Rule №2

The player scrolls through the world, not through a panel.

Любое вертикальное движение должно восприниматься как путешествие камеры, а не прокрутка списка.

Golden Rule №3

Mission cards belong to the active island.

Карточки заданий никогда не существуют сами по себе. Они появляются только возле текущего острова, визуально принадлежат ему и исчезают после завершения главы.
