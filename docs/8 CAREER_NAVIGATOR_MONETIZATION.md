# CAREER_NAVIGATOR_MONETIZATION

Version: 1.0

This document defines monetization structure, pricing logic, paywall strategy, and free vs premium boundaries.

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Monetization must never interrupt core progression.

The user journey toward employment remains primary.

Monetization appears only at natural value points.

---

# FREE VERSION

## Purpose

Allow users to experience real value before paying.

---

## Included in Free

* Onboarding
* Basic Career Roadmap (limited depth)
* First Chapter only (Resume Foundation)
* Limited tasks per day (max 2)
* Basic Career Score (simplified)
* Limited Interview Practice (1–2 sessions total)
* Basic Progress Tracking

---

## Free Limitations

* No access to full journey (chapters locked after Chapter 1)
* No advanced Interview Trainer modes
* No Playbook access
* No deep analytics
* No confidence gap analysis
* No profession expansion beyond default view

---

# PREMIUM VERSION

## Purpose

Unlock full career transformation system.

---

## Includes

* Full Career Journey (all chapters)
* Unlimited daily tasks
* Full Interview Trainer system
* Voice simulation modes (Practice / Simulation / Pressure Mode)
* Full Playbook access
* Full Career Engine analytics
* Confidence Gap Analysis
* All professions
* Advanced personalization logic
* Full progress tracking history

---

# LIFETIME ACCESS

## Positioning

One-time purchase for permanent access.

---

## Value Statement

“Pay once, use for every career change in your life.”

---

## Includes

Everything in Premium

No subscription required

All future updates included (version-based expansion)

---

# PRICING STRUCTURE (INITIAL RECOMMENDATION)

## Monthly Premium

Low entry barrier

---

## Lifetime

Main monetization focus

High perceived value

---

# PAYWALL STRATEGY

## When Paywall Appears

Only at high-value moments:

### 1. After first value experience

User completes onboarding + sees first roadmap

---

### 2. Before Chapter 2

Resume chapter completed → next chapter locked

---

### 3. Before Interview Trainer

First exposure → then lock advanced features

---

## Paywall should NOT appear:

* during onboarding
* during first task
* during confusion states
* during failure states

---

# PAYWALL MESSAGE STRATEGY

## Core Message

“Continue your journey to become interview-ready”

---

## Supporting Message

“You’ve completed your first step. Unlock the full path to your offer.”

---

## Value Focus

* structure
* clarity
* confidence
* guided path

---

# PSYCHOLOGICAL STRATEGY

## Principle 1: Progress interruption > forced sales

User pays when they feel blocked by value, not by pressure.

---

## Principle 2: Show progress before asking for money

User must feel invested in journey.

---

## Principle 3: Monetize transformation, not content

User is not buying lessons.

User is buying outcome:

“Get hired faster and with confidence.”

---

# VALUE PERCEPTION DESIGN

Premium is perceived as:

* Career system
* Structured roadmap
* Personal coach substitute
* Interview simulator
* Confidence builder

Not as:

* course
* content library
* app feature unlock

---

# RETENTION STRATEGY

## Goal

---

## Mechanism

* daily tasks
* streak system
* milestone unlocks
* career score progression

---
